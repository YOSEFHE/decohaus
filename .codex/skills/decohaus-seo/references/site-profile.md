## Search Console baseline on 2026-09-14

Search Console data was settled through 2026-09-11 at the time of review. The 28-day property summary showed 2 clicks and 8 impressions. This is an early-data baseline and must not be treated as statistically meaningful for trend decisions yet.

The submitted sitemap reported 20 submitted URLs and 0 indexed URLs in its aggregate content counter. However, direct Google URL Inspection on the following URLs returned `PASS` and `Submitted and indexed`:

- https://decohaus.ir/fa/
- https://decohaus.ir/en/
- https://decohaus.ir/fa/innovation/banaiq/
- https://decohaus.ir/en/innovation/banaiq/
- https://decohaus.ir/fa/projects/amirabad/

Therefore, do not interpret the sitemap's aggregate `indexed: 0` as proof that the site is not indexed. Prefer URL-level inspection when the two signals disagree.

## On-page audit baseline on 2026-09-14

A live audit of 10 pages found:

- 10/10 pages indexable
- 0 critical issues
- 0 high-severity issues
- 1 medium issue
- 12 low-severity issues
- Structured data present on audited pages
- No missing image alt text in the audited set
- H1 present on each audited page
- Hreflang present on audited pages

Notable findings:

- Persian contact page was flagged for very thin content (77 words). Improve only if additional useful contact/trust/service information genuinely helps the user; do not pad copy merely to hit a word count.
- Several English/project titles were slightly over ~60 characters and may truncate in SERPs. Rewrite only where the shorter title preserves intent and clarity.
- Home-page audit through `http://decohaus.ir` observed a redirect to `https://decohaus.ir/fa/` and a slow response in that specific request. Re-test before treating it as a persistent performance defect.
- Some home/services/BanaIQ pages were flagged as relatively thin (~200-280 words). Evaluate search intent and information gain before adding content.

## Editorial guardrails

- Persian is the primary editorial language; English pages should be native-quality adaptations, not mechanical translations.
- Avoid generic keyword-stuffed paragraphs.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives such as 'best', 'leading', or 'number one'.
- Do not publish confidential employer/client/project details, contract values, internal infrastructure, credentials, or private operational data.
- Keep project claims factual and verifiable.
- BanaIQ is in development/pilot positioning unless explicitly updated by the owner.

## Technical guardrails

- Preserve trailing-slash canonical conventions already used by the site.
- Keep canonical and hreflang pairs internally consistent.
- Verify both mobile rendering and source HTML for SEO-critical elements.
- Use structured data only when it reflects visible or verified facts.
- Any SEO change should include a post-change validation step.
